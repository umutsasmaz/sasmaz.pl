Learn the Polish you need every day.

Polski is our web app for learning Polish. It started as the app we use ourselves, and now we are opening it to other learners. Lessons, grammar, reading, speaking and a daily review all live in one place.

Open Polski Write to us

Live at polski.bizimeraketmeyin.com. English interface, Polish content.

A little practice every day

  • One daily review that mixes translation, cloze, inflection, dictation and sentence-building cards, scheduled by spaced repetition.
  • A streak, a daily goal and a mastery chart that shows how much you really know.
  • Devil words: the words that tripped you up in the last two weeks, kept in sight until they stop.
  • 30 lessons, from greetings to ordering a beer, each with its own deck.

Grammar that explains itself

  • A grammar map from A1 to C2, with articles for all seven cases, verbs, nouns, numbers, adjectives and pronouns.
  • Colour the endings to see the stem, the case or tense marker and the person ending in every example.
  • Drills for each case, and a case lab that declines any word you pick.
  • X-ray: paste any Polish text to see the case of every noun, the tense of every verb and the word that caused it, with lenses for conjugation, aspect and agreement.

Read, listen and speak

  • Short stories written for the app, four of them retelling Polish folk legends. Every word you already know is underlined as you read.
  • Daily news in Polish, paired paragraph by paragraph with English.
  • Select any Polish word, anywhere in the app, to see its dictionary card and add it to your deck.
  • Everyday dialogues to practise out loud, and word-by-word pronunciation feedback on any text you read aloud.

For life in Poland

News and official updates that matter to foreigners in Poland, from the sources you choose.

Culture

The flag, the anthem, the calendar and the unwritten rules: the context you cannot learn from flashcards.

On your phone
