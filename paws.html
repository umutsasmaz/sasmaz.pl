Everything a pet hotel runs on, in one place.

We built Paws 'N' Board for a pet hotel in Warsaw's Żoliborz district. Pet owners book and chat from their phone, the team sees the day's animals and tasks, and the owner runs the business from one panel.

Built, in testing before launch. Polish, English and Ukrainian.

For pet owners

  • Book a stay, daycare, a walk or a home visit, with add-ons, promo codes and packages, and see the full price before confirming.
  • Keep each pet's profile up to date: health, vaccinations, behaviour and special needs.
  • Chat with the team, send photos, and get walk reports and report cards.
  • See any cancellation fee before cancelling, join a waitlist when the hotel is full, and accept a quote.

For the team and the owner

  • Today at a glance: pets in the hotel, arrivals, departures and requests waiting for a decision.
  • Care checklists, tasks and medication rounds. Walks recorded with GPS and photos. QR check-in at the desk.
  • Rotas, shift swaps, time off, timesheets and payroll that follows Polish rules.
  • Customers, leads, packages, promotions and campaigns that respect consent and quiet hours. Reports with CSV and Excel export.

Built to be trusted

Private conversations

Chat between the hotel and pet owners is end-to-end encrypted. The owner and managers sign in with two steps.

Ready for GDPR

Customers can export their data or ask for it to be erased, and sensitive actions are written to an audit log.

Runs on a small server

One Docker stack on a small server behind a Cloudflare Tunnel, with nightly encrypted backups kept off site.

Where it stands

The whole system is built and running with demo data while we test it before launch.
